## 简介

面向美国军人：过去 12 个月内从现役退伍或退休的人，以及未来 12 个月内即将离开现役的军人。用 ID.me 验证后，可在个人 ChatGPT 账号上免费使用一年 ChatGPT Plus。Plus 包含 GPT-6：聊天里可用 GPT-6 Sol 的中档和高档思考，ChatGPT Work 和 Codex 里包含 GPT-6 Astra，另外还有更高的消息和上传额度、深度研究、图像生成、项目和自定义 GPT。每人限领一次，不能转赠，也不能和其他优惠叠加。企业工作区账号不能领。通过 App Store 或 Google Play 订阅的 Plus，要等移动端订阅结束后才能领。免费年结束后按原价续费，除非提前取消。

## Description

For U.S. military personnel: Individuals who have been discharged from active duty or retired within the past 12 months, as well as those who will leave active duty within the next 12 months. After verification via ID.me, you can use ChatGPT Plus for free for one year on your personal ChatGPT account. Plus includes GPT-6: mid-tier and high-tier thinking capabilities with GPT-6 Sol in chat, GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Work and Codex, as well as higher message and upload limits, in-depth research, image generation, projects, and custom GPTs. This offer is limited to one claim per person, cannot be transferred, and cannot be combined with other promotions. Enterprise workspace accounts are not eligible. For Plus subscriptions purchased through the App Store or Google Play, you must wait until your mobile subscription ends before claiming the offer. After the free year ends, your subscription will renew at the regular price unless canceled in advance.

## 如何领取

1. 用要领取优惠的个人 ChatGPT 账号登录，打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 选择 Verify with ID.me，完成军人身份验证，并允许把结果发给 OpenAI。如果没有返回退役日期，就按提示补上。
3. 页面显示资格确认后，点 Claim offer。已经在网页版订阅 Plus 的不要先取消，验证后会直接记上一整年。
4. 如果 Plus 是在 App Store 或 Google Play 买的，先在对应商店取消，等订阅结束后再回来领取。

## How to claim

1. Log in with the personal ChatGPT account you want to use for the offer and open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. Select Verify with ID.me, complete the military identity verification, and allow the results to be sent to OpenAI. If your discharge date is not returned, follow the prompts to provide it.
3. Once your eligibility is confirmed on the page, click Claim offer. If you are already subscribed to Plus via the web version, do not cancel first; your subscription will be extended by a full year after verification.
4. If your Plus subscription was purchased through the App Store or Google Play, cancel it first in the respective store, then return to claim the offer after your subscription has ended.
