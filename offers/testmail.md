## 简介

GitHub 学生包在读期间提供 Essential 方案（约 $9/月）：每月 1 万封测试邮件、完整 API、1 到 3 天保留和一个随机 namespace。教师、开源项目和非营利组织可写信申请教育或公益用途。

## Description

The GitHub Student Pack provides the Essential plan (about $9/month) during your studies: 10,000 test emails per month, a full API, a retention period of 1 to 3 days, and a random namespace. Teachers, open-source projects, and non-profit organizations can apply for educational or charitable use by sending an email.

## 如何领取

1. 学生先完成 GitHub Education 认证，再从学生包里的 Testmail 入口开通 Essential。
2. 教师、开源维护者或非营利组织发邮件到 support@testmail.app，说明教育或公益用途。

## How to claim

1. Students should first complete the GitHub Education verification, then activate the Essential plan through the Testmail portal within the Student Pack.
2. Teachers, open-source maintainers, or non-profit organizations should email support@testmail.app, explaining their educational or charitable purpose.
