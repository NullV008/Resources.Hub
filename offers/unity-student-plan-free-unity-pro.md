## 简介

年满 16 岁、在认证学校在读的学生可免费领取 Unity Student：最新 Unity Pro 编辑器、Odin Inspector/Validator 教育许可、Synty 资源包和 Unity Cloud。通过 SheerID 验证，许可一年，在读可续期。

## Description

Students 16+ at an accredited school can get Unity Student free: the latest Unity Pro Editor, an Odin Inspector and Validator education license, a Synty asset bundle, and Unity Cloud. Verify with SheerID. The license lasts one year and can be renewed while enrolled.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，按学段选择 Post-Secondary 或 Secondary。
2. 注册或登录 Unity ID（不必用学校邮箱）。
3. 用 SheerID 提交学籍信息；多数即时通过，补充材料会发邮件。
4. 查收许可密钥，在 Unity Hub 激活学生许可。
计划一年有效，到期或到期前 60 天可续；仅绑定一个 Unity ID。课堂机房请走学校 Education Grant，不要用学生计划批量装机。

## How to claim

1. Go to the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and select Post-Secondary or Secondary based on your education level.
2. Register or sign in with a Unity ID (a school email is not required).
3. Submit your enrollment information through SheerID; most are approved instantly, and requests for additional materials are sent by email.
4. Receive your license key and activate the student license in Unity Hub.
The plan is valid for one year and can be renewed at expiration or up to 60 days before it expires; it is tied to only one Unity ID. For classroom labs, use the school's Education Grant; do not use the student plan for bulk installations.
