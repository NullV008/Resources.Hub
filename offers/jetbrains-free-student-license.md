## 简介

认证院校的学生和教师可以免费使用 JetBrains 全套桌面 IDE，包括 IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate、PyCharm、WebStorm、GoLand、CLion 和 Rider。教育许可不能用于商业项目。在读期间每年续一次，毕业后两年内买个人订阅可享 6 折。

## Description

Students and faculty at accredited institutions can use the full suite of JetBrains desktop IDEs for free, including IntelliJ IDEA Ultimate, PyCharm, WebStorm, GoLand, CLion, and Rider. Educational licenses cannot be used for commercial projects. The license is renewed annually during your studies, and you can get a 40% discount on a personal subscription within two years of graduation.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱、ISIC/ITIC，或已认证的 GitHub 学生包提交申请。
2. 点邮件里的验证链接。通过后登录 JetBrains 账号下载 IDE，并在 IDE 里登录激活。
3. 每年要重新提交在读或在职证明。AI Assistant 只有试用，试用结束后是 AI 免费档或另付费。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and submit your application using your school email, ISIC/ITIC, or a verified GitHub student package.
2. Click the verification link in the email. Once verified, log in to your JetBrains account to download the IDE and log in within the IDE to activate it.
3. You must resubmit proof of enrollment or employment each year. The AI Assistant is only available as a trial; after the trial ends, you can use the free AI tier or pay for an upgrade.
