## 简介

Replit 是浏览器端 AI 编程与部署平台，支持随时随地编写、运行和部署代码。使用.edu 教育邮箱认证后，可享受 Core 会员 5 折优惠，仅需 $10/月（原价 $20/月）。适合正在构建作品集、个人项目和创业项目的学生。会员权益包括：完整 AI 额度的 Replit Agent（AI 自动编程助手）、无限私有应用（Private Apps）、生产级应用部署（Deploy）、更快的工作空间（Workspace）性能。

## Description

Replit is a browser-based AI coding and deployment platform for writing, running, and deploying code from anywhere. Verified students with a.edu email get 50% off the Core membership — just $10/month (regular $20/month). Ideal for students building portfolios, side projects, and startups. Benefits include: Replit Agent with full AI credits, unlimited private apps, production app deployment, and faster workspaces.

## 如何领取

1. 访问本页的「前往官网领取」。2. 使用学校颁发的.edu 邮箱注册或登录 Replit 账号。3. 按页面提示完成学生身份验证。4. 验证通过后，Core 会员将以 $10/月（5折）价格提供，包含完整 AI Agent 额度、无限私有应用、生产部署与更快工作空间。

## How to claim

1. Visit the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. Register or sign in to Replit with your school-issued.edu email.
3. Follow the on-page instructions to complete student verification.
4. After verification, Core membership is available at $10/month (50% off), including full AI Agent quota, unlimited Private Apps, production deployment, and faster Workspace.
