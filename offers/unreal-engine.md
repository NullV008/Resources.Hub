# 虚幻引擎学生许可

**Unreal Engine Student License**

- 官网域名 / Domain: unrealengine.com
- 源页面 / Source: [中文](https://edu.52it.de/zh/offer/unreal-engine) · [English](https://edu.52it.de/en/offer/unreal-engine)

## 简介

虚幻引擎对学生和教育免费。学生许可可以发布项目；如果总收入超过 100 万美元，才按虚幻引擎的版税规则支付。课程作业和个人作品一般碰不到这个门槛。

## Description

Unreal Engine is free for students and educational institutions. The student license allows you to publish projects; royalties under Unreal Engine's terms apply only if total revenue exceeds $1 million. Course assignments and personal projects typically won't reach this threshold.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用 Epic Games 账号登录。
2. 选择学生或学校许可并完成验证，然后再安装引擎。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and log in with your Epic Games account.
2. Select the student or school license, complete verification, and then install the engine.
