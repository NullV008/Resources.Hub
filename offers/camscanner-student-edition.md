## 简介

扫描全能王的学生认证通过后，可以使用高级扫描功能，例如去手写、转 Word 和更多云同步。免费时长以认证成功后的会员到期日为准。

## Description

After verifying your student status in CamScanner, you can use advanced scanning features such as handwriting removal, conversion to Word, and more cloud synchronization. The free period is based on the expiration date of your membership after successful verification.

## 如何领取

1. 登录后点击本页的「前往官网领取」，进入学生认证。
2. 按页面上传学生证或学信网证明。通过后再看高级会员的到期日。

## How to claim

1. After logging in, click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to start the student verification process.
2. Upload your student ID or Xuexin.com verification document as instructed on the page. Check the expiration date of your premium membership after verification is complete.
