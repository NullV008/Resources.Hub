**Lumion Educational Licenses**

## 简介

学生可免费使用一年 Lumion Student，功能对齐 Lumion Pro 2026，渲染带小水印，在读可续。教师许可 3 年，仅限学校机房，不能商用。

## Description

Students get Lumion Student free for one year, with Lumion Pro 2026 features and a small watermark, renewable while enrolled. Faculty licenses last 3 years, for campus labs only, and are not for commercial use.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，按学生或教师申请。
2. 学生许可 1 年，在读可续。作品不能用商业版打开，也不能商用。
3. 教师版只用于大学机房。先看系统要求。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and apply as a student or teacher.
2. Student license is valid for 1 year, renewable while enrolled. Projects cannot be opened with the commercial version and cannot be used commercially.
3. Teacher version is only for university computer labs. Check system requirements first.
