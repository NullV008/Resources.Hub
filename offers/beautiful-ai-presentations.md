## 简介

Beautiful.ai 用模板生成演示文稿。学生验证通过后可以免费使用专业版一年。

## Description

Beautiful.ai lets you create presentations using templates. Students can use the Pro version for free for one year after verification.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱注册。
2. 完成学生验证后再升级。看账户里的到期日，一年结束后会要求付费。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and register with your school email.
2. Upgrade after completing student verification. Check the expiration date in your account; you'll be prompted to pay after the one-year period ends.
