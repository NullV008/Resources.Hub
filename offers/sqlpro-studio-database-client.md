## 简介

SQLPro Studio 是 macOS 和 iOS 上的数据库客户端，学生可以按教育价购买，用来连接和管理多种数据库。

## Description

SQLPro Studio is a database client for macOS and iOS. Students can purchase it at an educational discount to connect to and manage multiple databases.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，查看当前学生价。
2. 用学校邮箱或学生证明下单。价格以结账页为准。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to check the current student price.
2. Place your order using your school email or student verification. The price is subject to the checkout page.
