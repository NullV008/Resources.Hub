# Mapbox 教育账户

**Mapbox for Education**

- 分类 / Category: [开发者工具 / Developer Tools](../docs/categories/dev.md)
- 状态 / Status: Active
- 官网域名 / Domain: mapbox.com
- 源页面 / Source: [中文](https://edu.52it.de/zh/offer/mapbox-for-education) · [English](https://edu.52it.de/en/offer/mapbox-for-education)

## 简介

用学校邮箱注册 Mapbox 即可按用量使用免费额度。官方写免费额度足够覆盖几乎所有学生项目，没有单独的无限教育许可。

## Description

Create a Mapbox account with a school email and use the usage-based free tier. Mapbox says that tier covers almost all student projects. There is no separate unlimited education license.

## 如何领取

1. 用学校邮箱在本页的「前往官网领取」注册。
2. 按免费额度做课程和作业，超出后按量计费。
3. 课程合作可按页面联系 Mapbox。

## How to claim

1. Register with a school email at the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. Use the free tier for courses and assignments; anything beyond that is billed by usage.
3. For course collaborations, contact Mapbox via the page.
