## 简介

腾讯 SSV 数字教育实验室和 WorkBuddy 团队面向全国在职教师的 AI 教学助理，不限地区和学段。审核通过后每人每月 2000 积分，每月 1 日自动补满，文章写明可连续领取 12 个月；账号本身长期有效。积分用完后仍可使用免费模型。2026 年 10 月至 12 月为试点期，2000 积分所有模型通用；试点期结束后，混元 4 单独 1600 积分，其他模型 400 积分，混元 3 暂时不限量。这套积分和个人版不互通，数据也不会自动同步。

## Description

The Tencent SSV Digital Education Lab and the WorkBuddy team are offering an AI teaching assistant to all working teachers across China, regardless of region or educational level. After approval, each teacher will receive 2000 points per month, automatically replenished on the 1st of each month. The article states that this benefit can be claimed continuously for 12 months, and the account itself remains valid long-term. Free models can still be used after the points are exhausted. The pilot period runs from October to December 2026, during which all models will be accessible with the 2000 points. After the pilot period, Hunyuan 4 will require 1600 points separately, while other models will cost 400 points. Hunyuan 3 will temporarily have no usage limit. This point system is not interchangeable with the personal edition, and data will not be automatically synchronized.

## 如何领取

1. 在职老师打开公告，点「阅读原文」填写申领表：doc.weixin.qq.com/forms/AJEAIQdfAAoAXcAJQZtABoCNHisaiAXTf。也可以在「腾讯SSV」公众号菜单选择「一起SSV」-「WB教育公益版」。
2. 填写教师身份信息。审核通过后 3 个工作日内开通。登录必须使用报名时登记的手机号，一个手机号对应一个教育公益版账号。
3. 电脑端打开 WorkBuddy 5.8 及以上版本，用手机号验证码登录，在账号列表中选择「教育公益版」。已经装过的升级即可；有区域培训通知时，按通知里的链接下载。
4. 小程序登录时选择「企业版」，再输入登记的手机号。如果当前登的是个人版，先退出再按这个方式登录。
5. 学校要批量开通，填写批量收集表后发到项目群，或发到 p_guibzhang@tencent.com。生成内容先人工核对再用于课堂。

## How to claim

1. Working teachers should open the announcement and click "Read Original" to fill out the application form: doc.weixin.qq.com/forms/AJEAIQdfAAoAXcAJQZtABoCNHisaiAXTf. Alternatively, select "Together SSV" > "WB Education Public Welfare Edition" from the menu of the official "Tencent SSV" WeChat account.
2. Fill in your teacher identification information. The account will be activated within 3 business days after approval. You must use the mobile number registered during sign-up to log in; each mobile number corresponds to one Education Public Welfare Edition account.
3. Open WorkBuddy version 5.8 or higher on your computer, log in with the verification code for your mobile number, and select "Education Public Welfare Edition" from the account list. If already installed, simply upgrade. When a regional training notice is issued, download the app via the link provided in the notice.
4. When logging in via the mini-program, select "Enterprise Edition" and then enter your registered mobile number. If you are currently logged into the personal edition, log out first and then follow this procedure to log in.
5. For schools wishing to activate accounts in bulk, fill out the bulk collection form and send it to the project group or to p_guibzhang@tencent.com. Content generated by the tool should be manually reviewed before use in the classroom.
