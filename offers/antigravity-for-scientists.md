## 简介

最多 1 万名入选的科研人员，可免费使用 Google Antigravity 最高用量档 6 个月，并使用 Gemini 4 Argon。面向在大学、可授学位的院校、非营利研究机构或国家实验室从事非商业科研的人，包括教师、实验室负责人、博后、博士和硕士。每周滚动审核，只通知入选的人，提交申请不代表一定通过。额度加在提交表单时登录的那个 Google 账号上，走的是个人版，不是学校的 Gemini Enterprise。

## Description

Up to 10,000 selected researchers will receive free access to the highest usage tier of Google Antigravity for 6 months, along with access to Gemini 4 Argon. This offer is for individuals engaged in non-commercial research at universities, degree-granting institutions, non-profit research organizations, or national laboratories, including faculty, lab directors, postdoctoral researchers, PhD students, and master's students. Applications are reviewed on a rolling weekly basis, and only selected applicants will be notified. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval. The allocation will be added to the Google account used to log in when submitting the form, under the personal version of the service, not the school's Gemini Enterprise account.

## 如何领取

1. 用打算接收额度的 Google 账号登录。个人 Gmail 和学校的 Workspace 账号都可以。学校账号要先确认能登录 Antigravity。
2. 在科学用途页点「在此申请」。填写所在机构、机构邮箱，以及实验室或课题组的公开网页。
3. 至少提供一个有效的 ORCID，或一个有效的 Google Scholar 主页。两个都没有的申请不会被考虑。
4. 用 3 到 4 句话说明未来 6 到 12 个月要做的研究，至少 300 个字符。不要写保密或专有内容。
5. 入选后，最高档会加到提交时登录的账号上，为期 6 个月。没有入选不会收到通知。

## How to claim

1. Log in with the Google account you want to receive the allocation. Both personal Gmail accounts and institutional Workspace accounts are acceptable. If using an institutional account, confirm first that it has access to Antigravity.
2. On the scientific use page, click "Apply here." Provide your institution, institutional email address, and a public webpage for your lab or research group.
3. Include at least one valid ORCID or a valid Google Scholar profile. Applications without either will not be considered.
4. Describe your research plans for the next 6 to 12 months in 3 to 4 sentences, totaling at least 300 characters. Do not include confidential or proprietary information.
5. If selected, the highest tier will be added to the account used during submission for a period of 6 months. Unsuccessful applicants will not receive a notification.
