## 简介

有高等教育机构邮箱的在读学生和教师，可免费领取 12 个月 Qt 教育许可，只用于学习，不能商用。可以两套都领：设计版包含 Qt Design Studio Enterprise，可从 Figma、Sketch、Adobe XD 导入界面；开发版包含 Qt Device Creation Professional，支持 Python、C++、JavaScript、HTML5 和 QML，可部署到树莓派以及部分 ST、NXP、瑞萨 MCU，并附带 Design Studio Professional。没有官方技术支持，可以看 Qt Academy 和论坛。做出的应用只能为了学习分享。到期后可以申请续期。

## Description

Current students and teachers with an email address from a higher education institution can receive a free 12-month Qt Education License for learning purposes only, not for commercial use. Both editions are available: the Design Edition includes Qt Design Studio Enterprise, allowing you to import interfaces from Figma, Sketch, and Adobe XD; the Development Edition includes Qt Device Creation Professional, supporting Python, C++, JavaScript, HTML5, and QML, with deployment capabilities to Raspberry Pi and select ST, NXP, and Renesas MCUs, along with Design Studio Professional. Official technical support is not provided, but resources such as Qt Academy and forums are available. Applications created must be shared solely for educational purposes. Renewal can be requested after the license expires.

## 如何领取

1. 打开申请页，用高等教育机构邮箱填表，并设置 Qt 账号密码。系统会自动核对邮箱是否属于已知院校。
2. 查收验证邮件并点击链接，垃圾箱也要看。
3. 按邮件说明安装。许可 12 个月，需要继续用时再申请续期。

## How to claim

1. Open the application page, fill out the form using your higher education institution's email address, and set up your Qt account and password. The system will automatically verify whether your email belongs to a recognized institution.
2. Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link. Also, check your spam folder.
3. Follow the instructions in the email to complete the installation. The license is valid for 12 months; you'll need to apply for renewal if you wish to continue using it.
