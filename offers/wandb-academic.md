## 简介

学生、教师和高校科研人员可以一直免费使用 W&B，用来记录实验、对比模型、做报告和团队协作，对接 PyTorch、Keras、JAX、Hugging Face 等。页面写明 Always free，不是限时券。课堂和实验室有入门材料，也有免费课程。学校若要办活动，可以在页面联系。论文里用了 W&B 的，官方希望引用并邮件告知。

## Description

Students, teachers, and university researchers can use W&B for free indefinitely to log experiments, compare models, create reports, and collaborate with teams, with integration for PyTorch, Keras, JAX, Hugging Face, and more. The page states it's 'Always free,' not a time-limited offer. Introductory materials and free courses are available for classrooms and labs. Schools wishing to host events can contact the page. If you use W&B in your papers, the official team would appreciate a citation and an email notification.

## 如何领取

1. 用学校身份点击本页的「前往官网领取」注册。学术免费按官网对学生、教师和高校科研人员开放。
2. 在训练脚本里接入实验记录。课堂或实验室可先看页面上的免费教程和课程。

## How to claim

1. Use your university affiliation to click 'Go to Official Website to Claim' on this page and register. The academic free plan is available to students, teachers, and university researchers as outlined on the official website.
2. Integrate experiment logging into your training scripts. For classrooms or labs, you can first review the free tutorials and courses on the page.
