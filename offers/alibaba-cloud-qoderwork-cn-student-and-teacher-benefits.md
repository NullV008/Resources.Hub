## 简介

QoderWork CN 是阿里云推出的桌面级 AI 智能体助手，支持通过对话完成 PPT 编写美化、论文排版查重、数据分析与可视化、代码生成与调试等学习任务。高校专属权益面向在校大学生与教师：通过身份核验后，在享受新用户首月免费 Pro（价值 59 元）的基础上，可额外获得 4,000 Credits（有效期 90 天）。学生通过支付宝连接学信网学籍信息完成实时核验；教师提交身份证明后 1-3 个工作日审核，权益在审核通过后 T+1 工作日自动发放。同一身份仅可绑定 1 个阿里云账号、领取 1 次权益。

## Description

QoderWork CN is Alibaba Cloud's desktop AI agent assistant for completing study tasks via conversation: PPT creation, paper editing, data analysis & visualization, and code generation. University students and teachers who pass identity verification receive a free first-month Pro membership (worth CNY 59) plus 4,000 additional Credits (valid 90 days). Students verify instantly via Alipay + Xuexin.com; teachers submit credentials for 1-3 business day review, with benefits issued T+1 day post-approval. Each identity may bind to only one Alibaba Cloud account and claim benefits once.

## 如何领取

步骤一：下载 QoderWork CN 客户端并使用阿里云账号登录，领取新用户首月免费 Pro 权益。
步骤二：返回认证页面本页的「前往官网领取」，使用同一阿里云账号登录。
步骤三（学生）：点击【学生认证 -> 立即领取】，按提示通过支付宝查询学信网学籍信息完成实时核验，核验成功后即可领取 4,000 Credits。
步骤三（教师）：点击【教师认证】，上传教师身份证明材料，等待 1-3 个工作日审核，审核通过后 T+1 工作日自动发放权益。
注意：同一身份只能绑定 1 个阿里云账号，仅可领取 1 次。近一个月内已完成认证的用户可直接领取，无需二次认证。

## How to claim

Step 1: Download the QoderWork CN client and log in with an Alibaba Cloud account to claim the new user first-month free Pro benefit.
Step 2: Return to the verification page the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and log in with the same Alibaba Cloud account.
Step 3 (Students): Click [Student Verification -> Claim Now], follow the prompts to query CHSI student status information via Alipay for real-time verification, and after successful verification, claim 4,000 Credits.
Step 3 (Teachers): Click [Teacher Verification], upload teacher identity proof materials, wait 1-3 working days for review, and benefits will be automatically issued on T+1 working day after approval.
Note: The same identity can only be bound to 1 Alibaba Cloud account and can only be claimed 1 time. Users who have completed verification within the past month can claim directly without a second verification.
