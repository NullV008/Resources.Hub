## 简介

学生和教师用学校邮箱注册后，多数情况会自动开通 PDF2Go 教育高级功能，只限非商用，在读或在职期间可免费续。

## Description

Students and teachers who sign up with a school email usually get PDF2Go educational premium tools automatically. Use is non-commercial, and the license can be renewed for free while you study or teach.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱点 Get started。
2. 学校能自动识别时，教育高级功能会直接开通；否则联系官方。
3. 不得用于商业产品，也不得把许可分享给他人。

## How to claim

1. Go to the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and click Get started with your school email.
2. If your school can be recognized automatically, education premium features will be activated directly; otherwise, contact the official team.
3. Do not use it for commercial products, and do not share the license with others.
