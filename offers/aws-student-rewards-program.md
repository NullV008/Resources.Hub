**AWS Student Rewards Program**

## 简介

AWS 学生福利计划：验证学生身份并完善资料，立即解锁12个月Skill Builder高级会员（价值$449），活跃赢徽章还可再得$30积分和$100认证考试券。

## Description

Verify your student status to unlock 12 months of AWS Skill Builder Premium free ($449 value), plus earn AWS credits and a $100 certification exam voucher through activity badges.

## 如何领取

1. 访问 builder.aws.com 并登录/注册 AWS Builder Center 账号（可用现有 AWS 账号登录）。
2. 在个人资料页找到 Student Status（学生状态）入口，点击进行 SheerID 学生身份验证，通常几分钟内完成（需年满 18 岁、在认可高校在读）。
3. 验证通过后，完善 Builder Center 个人资料：上传头像并填写 About 简介，完成后立即解锁 12 个月 AWS Skill Builder 高级会员（价值 $449）。
4. 在 Builder Center 保持活跃赢取徽章：发表文章、评论、参与 Wishlist 等操作可累积徽章。
5. 达到 7 个徽章解锁 $10 云积分；14 个徽章再得 $20；21 个徽章兑换 $100 的 AWS Foundational 认证考试代金券。
注意：该计划为 2026 返校季限时推出，具体权益与有效期以官方页面为准。

## How to claim

1. Visit builder.aws.com and log in/register for an AWS Builder Center account (you can use your existing AWS account to log in).
2. On your profile page, find the Student Status entry, click to verify your student status via SheerID, which usually takes a few minutes (you must be at least 18 years old and enrolled at an accredited college or university).
3. After verification, complete your Builder Center profile: upload a profile picture and fill in the About section. Once done, you will immediately unlock 12 months of AWS Skill Builder Premium membership (worth $449).
4. Stay active in Builder Center to earn badges: publishing articles, commenting, participating in Wishlist, and other activities can accumulate badges.
5. Reach 7 badges to unlock $10 in cloud credits; 14 badges to get another $20; 21 badges to redeem a $100 AWS Foundational certification exam voucher.
Note: This program is a limited-time offer for the 2026 back-to-school season. Specific benefits and expiration dates are subject to the official page.
