## 简介

学生和教师登录后填表，可以把 Amp Megawatt 从每月 $20 打成 $10。只打 Megawatt，Gigawatt 不参加。官方建议配合已有的 ChatGPT 或 X Premium+、SuperGrok 订阅使用，包含的 $10 用量只够额外试用。已订阅的人下个续费日才生效，不退已付的钱。

## Description

Students and teachers can log in, fill out a form, and reduce the monthly cost of Amp Megawatt from $20 to $10. This discount applies only to Megawatt; Gigawatt is not included. The official recommendation is to use this with an existing ChatGPT, X Premium+, or SuperGrok subscription, as the included $10 credit is only sufficient for an additional trial period. For existing subscribers, the discount will take effect on the next renewal date, and no refunds will be issued for payments already made.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，登录 Amp。
2. 按页面表格提交学生或教师身份。通过后 Megawatt 显示为每月 $10。
3. 如果已经在付费，折扣从下次续费开始，不会退之前的费用。活动可以随时调整或停止。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and log in to Amp.
2. Submit your student or teacher status via the form on the page. Once approved, Megawatt will be displayed at $10 per month.
3. If you are already paying, the discount will apply starting from your next renewal; no refunds will be provided for previous payments. This promotion is subject to change or termination at any time.
