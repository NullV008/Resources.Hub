1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page to learn about student/academic writing use cases.
2. The student page itself does not list discount figures. Official help states: eligible students, teachers, and professors can get up to about 20% off Premium with an education discount (help.languagetool.org/hc/en-us/articles/39254510973335-What-subscriptions-does-LanguageTool-offer).
