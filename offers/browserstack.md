## Description

The GitHub Student Developer Pack includes a one-year free license to BrowserStack, covering Live and Automate Mobile. This allows you to perform interactive and automated testing across over 3,000 desktop browsers and real Android and iOS devices, valued at approximately $2,388/year.

## 如何领取

1. 先完成 [GitHub Education](https://education.github.com/pack) 学生认证。
2. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，点击 Sign Up with GitHub。
3. 用已认证的 GitHub 账号授权登录，领取 Live 和 Automate Mobile 一年免费许可。

## How to claim

1. First, complete your student verification at [GitHub Education](https://education.github.com/pack).
2. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and click Sign Up with GitHub.
3. Use your verified GitHub account to authorize your login and claim your one-year free license to Live and Automate Mobile.
