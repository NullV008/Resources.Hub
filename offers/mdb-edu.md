**MDB Edu Student Discount**

## 简介

个人可用学校邮箱写信并核对学生证，首次购买任意 MDB 套餐享 5 折。学校在官网发布 MDB 指定教程并通过审核后，学生还能用学校邮箱免费使用高级组件 12 个月，仅限教育用途。

## Description

Individuals can apply by email with a school email address and verify their student ID to get 50% off their first purchase of any MDB plan. After the school publishes an MDB-designated tutorial on its official website and it passes review, students can also use advanced components free for 12 months with a school email address, for educational use only.

## 如何领取

要 5 折：用学校邮箱写信申请，按回复核对学生证。
要免费：请教师或图书馆按页面要求把教程发在学校域名上，并邮件通知 MDB，通过后用学校邮箱领取。

## How to claim

For 50% off: apply by email with your school email address, then verify your student ID according to the reply.
For free access: ask a teacher or the library to publish the tutorial on the school domain as required on the page, and email MDB to notify them; after approval, claim it with your school email address.
