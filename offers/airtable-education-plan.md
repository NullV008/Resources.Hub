**Airtable Education Plan**

## 简介

拥有.edu 邮箱的学生和教师注册 Airtable 账户并创建工作区后，即可立即获得 120 天团队版试用计划。

## Description

Students and educators with a.edu email address can get an immediate 120-day trial of the Team plan after signing up for an Airtable account and creating a workspace.

## 如何领取

1. 使用.edu 邮箱注册 Airtable 账号（airtable.com）
2. 创建一个新工作区
3. 系统自动识别教育邮箱并激活 120 天团队版试用
4. 详细说明参见：本页的「前往官网领取」

## How to claim

1. Sign up for an Airtable account with a.edu email (airtable.com)
2. Create a new workspace
3. The system automatically recognizes the education email and activates the 120-day Team plan trial
4. See details at: the "Get it on the official site" button on this page
