## 简介

用学校邮箱登录后，Scira Pro 结账价为 $9/月；普通邮箱为 $15/月。邮箱域名匹配即可，不需要优惠码。

## Description

Sign in with a school email and Scira Pro is $9 a month at checkout, versus $15 otherwise. The discount applies when the verified email matches an allowed domain. No coupon code.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 用学校邮箱注册或登录。
3. 结账页会显示 $9/月的 Pro 价。域名不在允许列表时仍是 $15/月。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. Sign up or sign in with a school email.
3. The checkout page will show the $9/month Pro price. If the domain is not on the allowed list, it is still $15/month.
