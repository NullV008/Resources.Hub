## 简介

Edison Scientific 是科研向的 AI 工作台。用 .edu 邮箱注册，每月有 10 次推理；首月再送 1300 次。

## Description

Edison Scientific is an AI workspace designed for scientific research. Register with a .edu email address to receive 10 inference requests per month; an additional 1,300 requests are provided during the first month.

## 如何领取

1. 用.edu 邮箱点击本页的「前往官网领取」注册。
2. 到账后先用首月的 1300 次。之后每月只有 10 次，用完要等下个月或付费。

## How to claim

1. Use a.edu email address to click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and register.
2. Once the quota is credited, use the 1,300 requests from the first month first. After that, you will only receive 10 requests per month. Once they are used up, you will need to wait until the next month or pay for additional usage.
