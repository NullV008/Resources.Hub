## 简介

高校全日制学生可申请 Linear Basic 或 Business 一年全免，用于学生项目和社团。教职工约 75% 折扣。高中、营利性和纯在线院校不符合。

## Description

Full-time college students can get Linear Basic or Business free for one year for student-led programs and clubs. University staff can get about 75% off. High schools, for-profit schools, and online-only schools are excluded.

## 如何领取

1. 用.edu 邮箱写 support@linear.app，附上工作区名称。
2. 学生需全日制在读证明（官方不接受单独学生证）；教职工需在职证明和学校认证证明。
3. 不能用于商业。非营利另有 75% 折扣，同样写信申请。

## How to claim

1. Write to support@linear.app from your.edu email, including your workspace name.
2. Students need proof of full-time enrollment (an official student ID alone is not accepted); faculty and staff need proof of employment and school verification.
3. Cannot be used for commercial purposes. Nonprofits have a separate 75% discount; apply by email as well.
