## Description

Verified students can use ElevenReader for free for one year (worth $99), converting textbooks, PDFs, papers, lecture notes, and web pages into natural speech with no usage limits. Features include read-along highlighting, making it ideal for individuals with ADHD, dyslexia, or those who struggle with prolonged reading. With over 1,000 voice options, it's available on iOS, Android, and web. Users must be at least 18 years old, currently enrolled in a school listed on the official list, and their ElevenLabs account email must match their school-issued email. After the one-year period, the subscription will automatically renew at the standard annual rate; to avoid this, cancel it in advance within your account's billing settings. No refunds are provided for existing paid subscriptions—redeem the offer after the current billing cycle ends. If your school isn't on the list, you can apply to be added on the page.
