## 简介

高校写作项目（含电影学院）在读学生可免费使用 Highland 2 完整版一年。用学校邮箱和学生证申请，通过后在 Mac 版里解锁。

## Description

Students in university writing programs, including film schools, can use the full Highland 2 free for one year. Apply with a school email and student ID, then unlock it in the Mac app.

## 如何领取

1. 在本页的「前往官网领取」提交姓名、.edu 邮箱、项目和学生证。
2. 审核邮件通过后，从 Mac App Store 下载 Highland 2。
3. 菜单里选 Upgrade to Pro，再用同一学校邮箱升级到学生版。一年后打印会带水印，除非付费升级。教师可写 support@quoteunquoteapps.com。

## How to claim

1. Submit your name, .edu email, program, and student ID at the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. After approval by email, download Highland 2 from the Mac App Store.
3. Select Upgrade to Pro from the menu, then upgrade to the student edition using the same school email. After one year, printouts will have a watermark unless you pay for an upgrade. Teachers can email support@quoteunquoteapps.com.
