## 简介

bolt.new 是 AI 编程工作区。学生验证后 Pro 五折；教师提交证明并审核通过后，可以获得一年 Pro。

## Description

bolt.new is an AI coding workspace. Students get 50% off Pro after verification; teachers can receive one year of Pro after submitting proof and passing review.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，学生提交教育邮箱，教师提交在职证明。
2. 等审核结果。学生是 Pro 五折，教师通过后是一年 Pro。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page. Students should submit their educational email, while teachers should submit proof of employment.
2. Wait for the review results. Students will receive 50% off Pro, and teachers will get one year of Pro upon approval.
