## 简介

用教育邮箱注册 iLovePDF，可以免费开通一年高级会员，用来合并、压缩、转换和签署 PDF。一年结束后要付费才能继续用高级功能。

## Description

Register for iLovePDF with an educational email address to unlock a free one-year premium membership for merging, compressing, converting, and signing PDFs. After the one-year period, you'll need to pay to continue using premium features.

## 如何领取

1. 用教育邮箱点击本页的「前往官网领取」注册。
2. 登录后确认高级会员已打开，并记下到期日。

## How to claim

1. Use your educational email address to click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and register.
2. After logging in, confirm that your premium membership has been activated and note the expiration date.
