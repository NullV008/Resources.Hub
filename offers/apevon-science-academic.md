## 简介

面向科研的 AI 智能体。高校教师和硕博研究生完成学术认证后，体验礼进会员卡包，自己选时候用：教师是 Plus 一个月（2 万积分，同时 3 个研究任务、2 个 GPU 任务），研究生是基础版一个月（5500 积分，同时 2 个研究任务、1 个 GPU 任务）。卡包长期有效，已有会员要等到期再用。月度积分每 30 天发一次、共 3 次（教师每次 1000、研究生每次 500），要到学术权益页领取，入账后 365 天有效。认证后 90 天内开通、续费或升级最多 3 次八折，失败不计次。本科生目前不能认证。

## Description

An AI agent designed for scientific research. After completing academic certification, university faculty and master's/PhD students can access the membership gift package and use it at their convenience: faculty receive one month of Plus membership (20,000 points, with 3 research tasks and 2 GPU tasks simultaneously), while graduate students receive one month of the Basic version (5,500 points, with 2 research tasks and 1 GPU task simultaneously). The membership package is valid long-term; existing members should wait until their current membership expires before using it. Monthly points are issued three times every 30 days (1,000 points per issuance for faculty, 500 points per issuance for graduate students) and must be claimed on the Academic Benefits page, where they remain valid for 365 days after crediting. Within 90 days of certification, you can activate, renew, or upgrade up to three times at a 20% discount; failed attempts do not count toward this limit. Undergraduate students are currently not eligible for certification.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，注册或登录。
2. 打开左下角「新手活动」，提交学术认证和材料。高校教师和硕博研究生是不同档，本科生目前没有。
3. 通过后先看会员卡包。已有会员要到期再用体验礼。月度积分到学术权益页点领取。付费套餐 90 天内最多三次八折。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to register or log in.
2. Open the "New User Activities" section in the bottom-left corner and submit your academic certification and supporting documents. University faculty and master's/PhD students are in different tiers; undergraduate students are not currently eligible.
3. After approval, review the membership gift package first. Existing members should wait until their current membership expires before using the gift. Monthly points must be claimed on the Academic Benefits page. Paid plans offer up to three 20% discounts within 90 days.
