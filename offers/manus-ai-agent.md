## 简介

Manus 是 AI 助手。学生注册不需要邀请码，可以直接获得 1000 积分，用于对话和创作。

## Description

Manus is an AI assistant. Students can register without an invitation code and receive 1000 credits directly for conversations and creation.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学生身份注册。
2. 注册完成后查看积分余额。1000 积分用完后的价格以 Manus 当前账单为准。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and register as a student.
2. Check your credit balance after registration. The price after the 1000 credits are used up will be based on Manus' current billing.
