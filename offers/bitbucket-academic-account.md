# Bitbucket 学术账户

**Bitbucket Academic Account**

- 分类 / Category: [开发者工具 / Developer Tools](../docs/categories/dev.md)
- 状态 / Status: Active
- 官网域名 / Domain: bitbucket.org
- 源页面 / Source: [中文](https://edu.52it.de/zh/offer/bitbucket-academic-account) · [English](https://edu.52it.de/en/offer/bitbucket-academic-account)

## 简介

用学术邮箱注册 Bitbucket，学生和教师可免费获得不限私有仓库，以及不限协作者。普通免费版协作者上限为 5 人。

## Description

Sign up for Bitbucket with an academic email and students and teachers get free unlimited private repositories plus unlimited collaborators. The regular free plan caps collaborators at five.

## 如何领取

1. 用学校邮箱注册 Bitbucket。域名在认证院校名单内会自动变成学术许可。
2. 没有自动开通时，按页面申请并补充学校信息。
3. 在 Settings → Plan Details 里确认 Current Plan 为 Academic。

## How to claim

1. Register Bitbucket with a school email. If the domain is on the list of accredited institutions, it will automatically become an academic license.
2. If it is not automatically activated, apply on the page and provide school information.
3. In Settings → Plan Details, confirm that Current Plan is Academic.
