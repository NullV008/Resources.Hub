## 简介

FlutterFlow 是用可视化方式做 Flutter 应用的平台。学生和教师可以按教育方案申请优惠，具体档位以价格页为准。

## Description

FlutterFlow is a platform for building Flutter apps visually. Students and teachers can apply for discounts under educational programs; specific tiers are subject to the pricing page.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，查看标了学生或教育的方案。
2. 用学校邮箱注册，并按页面完成身份验证后再升级。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to view plans labeled for students or education.
2. Register with your school email, complete identity verification on the page, and then upgrade.
