## 简介

Dify 是搭建 AI 应用和智能体的平台。教育用户可以申请教育专业版，通过后免费使用一年。

## Description

Dify is a platform for building AI applications and agents. Education users can apply for the Education Professional Edition and use it for free for one year upon approval.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱注册 Dify。
2. 按教育页提交申请，说明你是学生或教师。
3. 审核通过后开通一年教育专业版。到期前看页面是否还能续。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and register for Dify using your school email.
2. Submit an application on the education page, explaining that you are a student or teacher.
3. After approval, you will gain access to the Education Professional Edition for one year. Check the page before expiration to see if it can be renewed.
