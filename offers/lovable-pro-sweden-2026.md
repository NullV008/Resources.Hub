## 简介

在瑞典的大学、学院或国民高等学校就读、年满 18 岁的学生，用学校邮箱可以免费使用 Lovable Pro，从 2026 年 9 月 1 日到 12 月 31 日，含每月 100 积分。每人限一个工作区，不能和其他 Lovable 优惠叠加，也不含另外购买的积分。2027 年 1 月 1 日起自动改为学生五折。已经绑定付款方式的会开始扣费，不想继续要提前取消；没有付款方式的不会扣费。

## Description

Students aged 18 or older enrolled in a Swedish university, college, or national upper secondary school can use Lovable Pro for free with their school email address from September 1, 2026, to December 31, 2026, including 100 credits per month. Each person is limited to one workspace, and this offer cannot be combined with other Lovable discounts or additional purchased credits. Starting January 1, 2027, the offer will automatically switch to a 50% student discount. Users who have already linked a payment method will begin to be charged; if you no longer wish to continue, you must cancel in advance. Users without a linked payment method will not be charged.

## 如何领取

1. 打开活动页。还没有账号的，先用瑞典学校发放、目前仍可使用的邮箱注册。Lovable 账号邮箱必须是这个学校邮箱。
2. 在页面上验证并领取免费 Pro。2026 年 9 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日之间随时可以领。9 月 1 日之后领取的，从领取当天算到 12 月 31 日。
3. 工作区如果已经在付费的 Pro 1，已付的周期不退款。免费从当前付费周期结束时开始，最晚到 12 月 31 日。
4. 免费期结束后会自动套上学生五折。已绑定付款方式的，2027 年 1 月 1 日起按五折扣费，不想续费要在此之前取消。

## How to claim

1. Open the promotion page. If you don't have an account yet, first register using a school email address issued by a Swedish institution that is still valid. Your Lovable account must use this school email address.
2. Verify your email on the page and claim your free Pro subscription. You can claim it at any time between September 1 and December 31, 2026. If you claim it after September 1, the free period will start on the day of claiming and end on December 31.
3. If your workspace is already on the paid Pro 1 plan, the period you've already paid for will not be refunded. The free period will begin at the end of your current paid cycle, but no later than December 31.
4. After the free period ends, the 50% student discount will be automatically applied. For users who have linked a payment method, charges will be applied at a 50% discount starting January 1, 2027. If you do not want to renew, you must cancel before this date.
