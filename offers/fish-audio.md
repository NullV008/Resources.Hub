## 简介

认证高校的本科生和研究生，用 .edu 邮箱申请后可以领取免费语音额度，用来做课件配音、课程项目和黑客松。只限教育和非商业用途。支持约 83 种语言，也可以用大约 10 秒录音做声音克隆。额度数量以审核结果为准。

## Description

Undergraduate and graduate students at accredited universities can apply using a .edu email address to receive a free voice quota for creating course audio, project work, and hackathons. Limited to educational and non-commercial use only. Supports approximately 83 languages, and you can also use a roughly 10-second recording for voice cloning. The quota amount is subject to approval results.

## 如何领取

1. 用.edu 邮箱点击本页的「前往官网领取」申请学生额度。
2. 等审核。通过后再用于课程、演示或比赛，不要拿去商用。

## How to claim

1. Use a .edu email address to click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to apply for the student quota.
2. Wait for approval. Use it for courses, presentations, or competitions after approval—do not use it for commercial purposes.
