## 简介

Goodnotes 教育版在普通笔记之外，包含手写拼写检查、数学转换、数学助手、无限文件夹和手写搜索，并可以同步到 iCloud、Google Drive、OneDrive、Dropbox 或 WebDAV。

## Description

In addition to regular note-taking features, Goodnotes Education Edition includes handwriting spell check, math conversion, a math assistant, unlimited folders, and handwriting search. It also supports synchronization with iCloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or WebDAV.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱申请教育版。
2. 通过后再安装或登录 App。教育功能绑在通过验证的账号上。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and use your school email to apply for the Education Edition.
2. After approval, install or log in to the app. The educational features are linked to the verified account.
