## 简介

认证院校师生用学校邮箱申请，可免费用 6 个月 Tella Pro（无限录屏、AI 剪辑、106 种语言转写、最高 4K）。到期不自动扣费；之后仍符合资格可享 5 折。

## Description

Students and educators at accredited schools can get 6 months of Tella Pro free (unlimited recording, AI editing, transcription in 106 languages, up to 4K). It does not auto-charge. Eligible users can keep 50% off afterward.

## 如何领取

1. 用学校邮箱注册 Tella。
2. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，提交姓名、学校网站和学校邮箱。
3. 审核通常数天。通过后当前工作区获得 6 个月 Pro，每个工作区限一次，免费期不用绑卡，到期不自动续费。
4. 结束前 30 天可在账单里选 Pro 并绑卡，5 折从免费期结束后开始计费。每 12 个月需重新确认资格，符合条件即可一直享折扣。

院系多席位可写 sales+education@tella.com。

## How to claim

1. Sign up for Tella with your school email.
2. Go to the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and submit your name, school website, and school email.
3. Review usually takes a few days. Once approved, the current workspace gets 6 months of Pro, limited to one per workspace. No card required during the free period, and it won't auto-renew at expiry.
4. In the 30 days before it ends, you can select Pro in billing and add a card; billing at 50% off starts after the free period ends. Eligibility must be reconfirmed every 12 months; as long as you qualify, you keep the discount.

For multiple seats for a department, email sales+education@tella.com.
