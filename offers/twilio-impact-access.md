## 简介

501(c)(3) 及其对应的境外慈善组织、社会企业，可申请 Twilio 的短信、语音等产品折扣，并获得不会过期的免费用量，官方说足够发出数千条短信和通话。不要求签月度合约。超过 1200 万个组织可通过 Goodstack 快速审核。具体额度和折扣以批准信为准。

## Description

501(c)(3) organizations and their corresponding foreign charities and social enterprises can apply for discounts on Twilio's SMS, voice, and other products, as well as receive free usage that never expires—officially stated to be enough to send thousands of SMS messages and make calls. No monthly contract is required. Over 12 million organizations can undergo a fast-track review through Goodstack. Specific quotas and discounts are subject to the approval letter.

## 如何领取

1. 组织联系人点击本页的「前往官网领取」，按资格说明提交申请。
2. 一般 2 到 5 个工作日收到结果。通过后免费用量不会过期。
3. 先用免费额度做第一条短信或通话，再按折扣价扩大用量。

## How to claim

1. The organization's contact person should click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page and submit an application according to the eligibility criteria.
2. Results are typically received within 2 to 5 business days. Once approved, the free usage will not expire.
3. First, use the free quota for your initial SMS or call, then expand your usage at the discounted rate.
