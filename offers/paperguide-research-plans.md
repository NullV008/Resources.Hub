## 简介

Paperguide 用来检索、整理和写文献。免费档含每月 2,000 AI 点数；面向学生和独立研究者的 Plus 为年付约 $12/席/月。页面没有单独的学生免费许可。

## Description

Paperguide searches, organizes, and drafts research. The free plan includes 2,000 AI credits a month. Plus, aimed at students and independent researchers, is about $12 per seat per month billed annually. There is no separate free student license on the pricing page.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 可先用免费档。学生向方案是 Plus（年付展示价约 $12/席/月），不是学校邮箱自动免单。
3. 价格以结算页为准。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. You can start with the free tier. The student-oriented plan is Plus (displayed annual price about $12/seat/month), it is not automatically free with a school email.
3. Prices are subject to the checkout page.
