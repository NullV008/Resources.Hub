## 简介

美国大学在读学生用学校邮箱注册，自动获得 $20 Thunder Compute 额度，可用于 A6000 或 L40 GPU。用完后按正常按量计费。

## Description

Students at a US university who sign up with a school email get $20 in Thunder Compute credit automatically, usable on A6000 or L40 GPUs. After it runs out, normal pay-as-you-go billing applies.

## 如何领取

1. 用美国大学邮箱在本页的「前往官网领取」注册。
2. 域名核验通过后自动到账 $20，无需另外申请。
3. 额度可用于课程、科研或个人项目；用完后按量付费。滥用可能被收回。

## How to claim

1. Sign up at the "Get it on the official site" button on this page with a US university email.
2. Once the domain is verified, $20 is credited automatically; no separate application is needed.
3. The credit can be used for coursework, research, or personal projects; after it runs out, you pay as you go. Abuse may result in the credit being revoked.
