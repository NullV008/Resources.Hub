## 简介

在读学生和教师（含教授）用学校邮箱可免费注册 Exploratory 学术计划，含桌面端数据分析，以及共享服务器上的项目分享和 500MB 空间。

## Description

Current students and teachers, including professors, can sign up free for the Exploratory Academic plan with a school email. It includes Exploratory Desktop plus project sharing and 500MB on the shared server.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 选择 Student 或 Teacher，填写学校邮箱和学校名称。
3. 邮箱无法识别时写 support@exploratory.io。课堂和学业研究可用；机构运营类用途需要付费许可。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. Select Student or Teacher, and enter your school email and school name.
3. If your email isn't recognized, email support@exploratory.io. Available for classroom and academic research use; institutional operational use requires a paid license.
