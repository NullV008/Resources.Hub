## 简介

中国大陆在读大学生可以申请网易 VIP 邮箱。活动页还会附送网易云音乐、严选或云笔记等会员，具体礼包以当季页面为准。

## Description

Currently enrolled university students in mainland China can apply for a NetEase VIP email account. The event page also includes complimentary memberships to NetEase Cloud Music, Yanxuan, or Cloud Notes. The specific gift package is subject to the current season's page.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，准备好学信网或学校邮箱等在读证明。
2. 按页面完成学生认证后再领取邮箱和附赠会员。礼包内容会变，以提交时页面列出的为准。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and prepare proof of enrollment, such as your Xuexin.com profile or school email address.
2. Complete the student verification on the page, then claim your email account and complimentary memberships. The gift package contents may vary and are subject to what is listed on the page at the time of submission.
