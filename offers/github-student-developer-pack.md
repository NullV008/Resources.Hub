**GitHub Student Developer Pack**

## 简介

年满 13 岁、正在攻读学位或文凭的学生，通过 GitHub Education 认证后，可以领取合作方的开发工具、云额度和域名。教师不能领这个包。认证大约每两年要重做一次，很多合作方优惠从你开通的那天起算，不能反复领。

## Description

Students aged 13 and older who are currently pursuing a degree or diploma can receive developer tools, cloud credits, and domain names from partners after verifying their status through GitHub Education. Teachers are not eligible for this pack. Verification must be repeated approximately every two years. Many partner offers are valid from the date of enrollment and cannot be claimed multiple times.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用个人 GitHub 账号申请。有学校邮箱就先在账号里验证这个邮箱。
2. 学校不发邮箱，或域名认不出来时，上传带日期的学生证、课表、成绩单或在读证明。
3. 通过后再从学生包里逐个打开合作方。每一个都要单独领，不是认证完自动到账。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page and apply using your personal GitHub account. If you have a school email address, verify it in your account first.
2. If your school does not provide an email address or the domain cannot be verified, upload a student ID with a date, a class schedule, a transcript, or proof of enrollment.
3. After verification, open each partner's offer individually from the Student Pack. Each offer must be claimed separately; they will not be automatically added after verification.
