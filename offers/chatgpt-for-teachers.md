## 简介

面向美国认证 K–12 学校或学区在职的教师、职员、校领导和学区管理员，学生不能申请。验证学校邮箱后，可以使用面向课堂的 ChatGPT 工作区，免费到 2028 年 6 月。帮助中心目前列出的能力包括 GPT-5.1（Instant、Auto、Reasoning-Mini）、文件上传、深度研究、语音、图像生成、自定义 GPT，以及 SSO、角色权限和分析。默认不把内容用于训练模型，并提供面向学校的隐私保护。一个工作区只能放同一个学区的人。

## Description

Available to active teachers, staff, school leaders, and district administrators at U.S.-accredited K–12 schools or school districts. Students are not eligible. After verifying your school email, you can access the classroom-focused ChatGPT workspace for free until June 2028. The capabilities currently listed in the Help Center include GPT-5.1 (Instant, Auto, Reasoning-Mini), file uploads, deep research, voice, image generation, custom GPTs, as well as SSO, role-based permissions, and analytics. Content is not used for model training by default, and school-specific privacy protections are provided. Only individuals from the same school district can be added to a single workspace.

## 如何领取

1. 打开 [本页的](https://xn--8pvn55a0tz/)「前往官网领取」，用学校或学区邮箱开始验证。
2. 通过后选择新建本校或本学区的工作区，或加入域名下已有的工作区，并填写职务、学科和年级。
3. 管理员可以邀请同区已验证的同事，并配置 SSO 和权限。不能把其他学区的老师加进来。
4. 验证页报错时，换一个浏览器，用同一个 ChatGPT 账号重试。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and start verification using your school or district email.
2. After verification, choose to create a new workspace for your school or district, or join an existing workspace under your domain, and fill in your role, subject, and grade level.
3. Administrators can invite verified colleagues from the same district and configure SSO and permissions. Teachers from other districts cannot be added.
4. If you encounter an error on the verification page, try again using a different browser with the same ChatGPT account.
