## 简介

很多学校已经买了 Microsoft 365，用学校账号登录就能用 Word、Excel、PowerPoint 和 OneDrive。学校买了带 Copilot 的订阅才会有 Copilot。没有校园授权的学生，可以在微软学生页买 Microsoft 365 个人版学生价（大约半价，含 Copilot）。以前那 12 个月免费已经结束。

## Description

Many schools have already purchased Microsoft 365. You can use Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneDrive by signing in with your school account. Copilot is only available if the school has purchased a subscription that includes it. Students without campus licensing can purchase Microsoft 365 Personal at the student price (approximately half price, including Copilot) on the Microsoft Student page. The previous 12-month free trial has ended.

## 如何领取

1. 先用学校账号打开 office.com，看本校是否已经开通。
2. 没有校园授权再点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱验证学生身份。
3. 结账前确认是学生价。一年免费已经结束，以页面显示为准。

## How to claim

1. First, open office.com with your school account to check if your school has already activated it.
2. If there is no campus licensing, click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page and verify your student status using your school email.
3. Confirm that you are getting the student price before checkout. The one-year free trial has ended; please refer to the information displayed on the page.
