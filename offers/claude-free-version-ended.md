## 简介

东北大学、伦敦政治经济学院、尚普兰学院等学校曾经向本校账号提供免费 Claude。该校内通道已结束，现在不能按旧入口领取。

## Description

Schools such as Northeastern University, the London School of Economics and Political Science, and Champlain College previously offered free Claude access to their student accounts. This on-campus access has now ended, and it is no longer possible to obtain it through the old entry point.

## 如何领取

这个福利已经结束。不要再按旧教程注册或向同学索要入口。是否还有本校合作，以学校自己的通知为准。

## How to claim

This benefit has ended. Do not follow the old registration instructions or ask classmates for access. For any ongoing partnerships with your school, please refer to official announcements from the school itself.
