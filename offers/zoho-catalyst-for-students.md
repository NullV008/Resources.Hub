## 简介

学生可免信用卡在 Catalyst 上部署非商业项目。每 6 个月免费用量上限 $250，到顶会暂停而不会自动扣费，可向支持申请延长。

## Description

Students can deploy non-commercial apps on Catalyst with no card. Free usage is capped at $250 per six months. Hitting the cap pauses the app instead of charging you; Support can extend genuine student projects.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」用学生身份开始。
2. 无需订阅或信用卡，用于学习和非商业项目。
3. 6 个月内用量到 $250 会暂停。需要更多额度时联系 Catalyst Support，说明项目用途。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and start as a student.
2. No subscription or credit card required; for learning and non-commercial projects.
3. Usage reaching $250 within 6 months will pause. When you need more quota, contact Catalyst Support and explain your project purpose.
