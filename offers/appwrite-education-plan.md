## 简介

GitHub 学生包可免费使用 Appwrite Education 六个月：2 个云项目，用量与 Pro 相同（不含邮件支持），价值约 $40/月。每个账号只能领一次，不能用于商业用途。

## Description

GitHub Student Pack allows free use of Appwrite Education for six months: 2 cloud projects with the same usage as the Pro plan (excluding email support), valued at approximately $40/month. Each account can claim this benefit only once and it cannot be used for commercial purposes.

## 如何领取

1. 先完成 [GitHub Education](https://education.github.com/pack) 学生认证。
2. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，通过教育计划页面注册。
3. 系统核对学生包资格后自动套用 Education 方案，从加入当天起算 6 个月。

## How to claim

1. First, complete your student verification at [GitHub Education](https://education.github.com/pack).
2. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and register through the education program page.
3. After the system verifies your eligibility for the student pack, the Education plan will be automatically applied, starting from the day you join, for a duration of 6 months.
