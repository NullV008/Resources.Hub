## 简介

合格非营利组织和 K-12 学校可买 Claude Team 优惠价。页面展示约 $8/人/月，低收入和中等收入国家约 $3/人/月，席位约 2–150。需 Goodstack 核验。

## Description

Eligible nonprofits and K-12 schools can buy Claude Team at a nonprofit rate. The page shows about $8 per user per month, or about $3 in low- and middle-income countries, for roughly 2–150 seats. Verify with Goodstack.

## 如何领取

领取步骤

1.  打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2.  20 人以下的组织用 Goodstack 核验（约 2–3 分钟）；更大的组织联系销售。
3.  资格含美国 501(c)(3) 及国际同等组织、公立和私立 K-12，以及部分基层医疗机构。价格不含税，以结账页为准。

## How to claim

How to claim

1.  Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2.  Organizations with fewer than 20 people verify with Goodstack (about 2–3 minutes); larger organizations contact sales.
3.  Eligibility includes U.S. 501(c)(3) and international equivalent organizations, public and private K-12, and some community health centers. Prices exclude tax and are subject to the checkout page.
