**Roboflow Research Plan Quota**

## 简介

用学术邮箱、做非商业科研或教学，可申请 Research Plan：每月 50 额度、15 名成员、20 个项目、增强增强，以及 RF-DETR 研究许可（非商业）。申请时，该工作区里的数据会公开到 Roboflow Universe，不要放不能公开的数据。已在付费或试用付费套餐的人看不到申请按钮，要先取消试用。课堂需求可另找官方说明是师生项目。这是审核额度，不是无上限算力。

## Description

If you're using an academic email address for non-commercial research or educational purposes, you can apply for the Research Plan: 50 quota per month, 15 members, 20 projects, enhanced features, and an RF-DETR research license (non-commercial). When you apply, the data in this workspace will be made public on Roboflow Universe, so do not include any data that cannot be shared publicly. Users already on a paid plan or a paid trial will not see the application button; you must first cancel your trial. For classroom needs, you can contact official support separately and specify that it is a teacher-student project. This is a reviewed quota, not unlimited computing power.

## 如何领取

1. 用学术邮箱登录一个工作区。不要把不能公开的数据放进去。
2. 打开设置里的 Plan & Billing，拉到「Using Roboflow for research or education?」，点 Request Access。看不到按钮就先取消付费试用，再点击本页的「前往官网领取」看说明。
3. 选科研计划并确认升级。课堂定制需求按页面说明联系官方，并写明师生项目。

## How to claim

1. Log in to a workspace using an academic email address. Do not include any data that cannot be shared publicly.
2. Open Plan & Billing in the settings, scroll down to "Using Roboflow for research or education?", and click Request Access. If you don't see the button, first cancel your paid trial, then click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page to view the instructions.
3. Select the research plan and confirm the upgrade. For customized classroom needs, contact official support as described on the page and specify that it is a teacher-student project.
