# 通义听悟高校公益

**Tongyi Tingwu University Public Welfare Program**

- 分类 / Category: [效率工具 / Productivity](../docs/categories/productivity.md)
- 状态 / Status: Active
- 官网域名 / Domain: tingwu.aliyun.com
- 源页面 / Source: [中文](https://edu.52it.de/zh/offer/alibaba-cloud-tingwu-education-program) · [English](https://edu.52it.de/en/offer/alibaba-cloud-tingwu-education-program)

## 简介

阿里云通义听悟的高校公益计划把存储扩到 200 GB，并送 500 小时转写。只给符合页面要求的高校用户，用完不会自动再送。

## Description

Alibaba Cloud's Tongyi Tingwu university public welfare plan expands storage to 200 GB and includes 500 hours of transcription. This offer is exclusively for university users who meet the requirements on the page; no additional hours will be automatically provided after usage.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用阿里云或学校要求的账号登录。
2. 完成高校认证后再看存储和转写时长。500 小时是总额，不是每月 500 小时。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and log in with your Alibaba Cloud account or the account required by your university.
2. Complete university verification to view your storage and transcription time. The 500 hours is a total allowance, not 500 hours per month.
