## 简介

Anthropic 面向高校及非营利科研机构学者免费提供 Claude Team 标准席位，含 Claude Science/Code/Cowork，Premium 席位仅$15/月（原价$100）。

## Description

Anthropic offers verified PIs at universities and nonprofit research institutes free Claude Team Standard seats (Claude Science, Code, Cowork), with Premium seats at $15/mo (reg. $100).

## 如何领取

1. 访问本页的「前往官网领取」，点击 Verify now。
2. 以课题组负责人（PI）身份填写机构信息和研究方向，审核约需5-7个工作日。
3. 通过后邀请团队成员加入，Standard席位免费，Premium席位$15/月，限时优惠12个月。

## How to claim

1. Visit the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and click Verify now.
2. Fill in your institution's details and research area as a principal investigator (PI); review takes about 5-7 business days.
3. Once approved, invite team members to join: Standard seats are free, Premium seats are $15/month, limited-time offer for 12 months.
