## 简介

夸克 2026 开学季活动：大学生、教师完成教育认证可各领 3 个月夸克网盘 VIP + 3 个月扫描王 VIP（至 9 月 30 日）。教师节（9/8–9/12）教师经「夸克教育计划」支付宝小程序还可额外领扫描王年卡，最高合计 18 个月。

## Description

Quark 2026 back-to-school: verified students and teachers get 3 months Quark Drive VIP + 3 months Scan VIP (until Sep 30). Teachers can claim an extra Scan VIP annual card via the Quark Education Alipay mini program on Sep 8–12 (up to 18 months total).

## 如何领取

1. 打开夸克 APP，进入个人中心或搜索「教育认证」，进入开学季活动页；也可访问活动链接或「夸克教育计划」支付宝小程序。
2. 选择身份：大学生（学信网学籍核验）或教师（教师资格证生效核验）。
3. 完成认证后领取 3 个月夸克网盘 VIP + 3 个月夸克扫描王 VIP（活动至 9 月 30 日，每用户限领一次）。
4. 教师节专享（9 月 8 日–12 日）：教师通过「夸克教育计划」支付宝小程序完成认证，可额外领取扫描王 VIP 年卡；叠加后最高约 18 个月会员。

注意：具体权益与截止时间以官方活动页为准。

## How to claim

1. Open the Quark app, go to the personal center or search for 'Education Verification' to enter the Back-to-School Season activity page; you can also visit the activity link or the 'Quark Education Program' Alipay Mini Program.
2. Choose identity: college student (CHSI student status verification) or teacher (teacher qualification certificate validity verification).
3. After completing verification, claim 3 months of Quark Cloud Drive VIP + 3 months of Quark Scan King VIP (activity until September 30, limited to one claim per user).
4. Teachers' Day exclusive (September 8–12): teachers who complete verification via the 'Quark Education Program' Alipay Mini Program can additionally claim a Quark Scan King VIP annual card; after stacking, up to about 18 months of membership.

Note: Specific benefits and deadlines are subject to the official activity page.
