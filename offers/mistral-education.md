## 简介

学生和教师用学校或教育机构邮箱验证后，可以按教育价订阅 Mistral Pro。个人邮箱不行，学生证和在读证明也不能代替机构邮箱。折扣幅度以结算页为准。

## Description

Students and teachers can subscribe to Mistral Pro at the education rate after verifying their school or educational institution email address. Personal email addresses are not eligible, and student IDs or proof of enrollment cannot replace an institutional email. The discount amount is subject to the checkout page.

## 如何领取

1. 准备学校或教育机构发的邮箱。Gmail、iCloud、Outlook 这类个人邮箱不能用。
2. 新账号直接用这个邮箱注册。已有账号的，先把账号邮箱改成机构邮箱。
3. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，按教育方案订阅 Pro。
4. 邮箱域名认不出来时，把域名发给 Mistral 客服。学校不发邮箱的，不能申请。

## How to claim

1. Prepare an email address issued by your school or educational institution. Personal email services like Gmail, iCloud, and Outlook are not acceptable.
2. Use this institutional email to register a new account. If you already have an account, first change the account email to your institutional email.
3. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and subscribe to Pro under the education plan.
4. If the email domain cannot be recognized, send the domain to Mistral customer support. If your school does not provide an institutional email, you are not eligible to apply.
