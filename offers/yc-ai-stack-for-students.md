## 简介

参加 YC 高校活动后，可领取价值超过 $25,000 的学生免费额度：AWS、Azure 各 $10,000，OpenAI $1,000，Anthropic $500，以及多家 YC 公司的 AI 开发工具。资格自活动日起一年。

## Description

After a YC university event, students can redeem over $25,000 in free credits: $10,000 AWS, $10,000 Azure, $1,000 OpenAI, $500 Anthropic, plus AI tools from YC companies. Access lasts one year from the qualifying event.

## 如何领取

1. 参加 YC 高校活动（官方写 Fall 2025 起）。
2. 活动后查收确认邮件。
3. 用报名时的 Hacker News 账号登录本页的「前往官网领取」，资格会自动识别。
4. 逐项兑换云与 AI 额度。权益从活动日起一年。问题可写 deals@ycombinator.com。

## How to claim

1. Attend a YC university event (officially listed as starting Fall 2025).
2. After the event, check for a confirmation email.
3. Log in at the "Get it on the official site" button on this page with the Hacker News account you used to sign up; eligibility is recognized automatically.
4. Redeem the cloud and AI credits one by one. Benefits last one year from the event date. Questions can be sent to deals@ycombinator.com.
