## 简介

NoteGPT 用来总结 YouTube 等视频。学生可以免费使用一个月，之后是否收费以定价页为准。

## Description

NoteGPT is used to summarize videos from YouTube and other platforms. Students can use it for free for one month. Whether it will be paid afterward is subject to the pricing page.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，注册并找到学生或教育方案。
2. 完成验证后看账户里的到期日。一个月结束前如果不再使用，记得取消。

## How to claim

1. Click 「Go to Official Website to Claim」 on this page, register, and find the student or education plan.
2. After completing verification, check the expiration date in your account. If you no longer plan to use it before the month ends, remember to cancel.
