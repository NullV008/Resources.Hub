## 简介

面向美国大学的学术实验室。由 PI 或实验室负责人申请，审核通过后最多 30 个 ChatGPT Business Standard 席位可免费使用 12 个月，并在可信访问批准后使用生命科学模型 GPT-Rosalind。工作区里也能用 ChatGPT 和 Codex，不含 API。研究数据默认不用于训练，但没有零数据保留。这和 ChatGPT for Academic Researchers 不是同一个计划。

## Description

For academic labs at U.S. universities. Applications are submitted by the PI or lab director. Upon approval, up to 30 ChatGPT Business Standard seats can be used free of charge for 12 months, and the life sciences model GPT-Rosalind can be used after trusted access is approved. ChatGPT and Codex are also available in the workspace, but the API is not included. Research data is not used for training by default, but there is no zero-data retention policy. This is not the same program as ChatGPT for Academic Researchers.

## 如何领取

1. 确认实验室在美国的大学，并且学校允许单独开通 ChatGPT Business 工作区。学校已经签了 OpenAI Enterprise 协议的，改为联系学校管理员，走 Enterprise 申请。
2. 由 PI 或实验室负责人用绑定学校.edu 邮箱的 ChatGPT 账号，在公告页提交学术实验室申请。填写学校、实验室、职务和预期用途，并完成身份核验。
3. 准备一位学校指定的生物安全联系人，例如环境健康与安全、研究安全或生物安全官员。
4. 批准后按邮件完成工作区设置。开通时需要绑定付款卡。再把使用同一学校邮箱域名的成员加进来，并单独打开他们的 GPT-Rosalind 权限。
5. 免费期最长 12 个月，一个工作区最多 30 席。实验室成员不能单独申请。到期后的价格以获批时的协议为准。

## How to claim

1. Confirm that the lab is at a U.S. university and that the institution allows separate activation of a ChatGPT Business workspace. If the institution has already signed an OpenAI Enterprise agreement, contact the school administrator instead and proceed with the Enterprise application process.
2. The PI or lab director must submit the academic lab application on the announcement page using a ChatGPT account linked to a school.edu email address. Provide the school, lab, role, and intended use, and complete identity verification.
3. Prepare a biosecurity contact designated by the school, such as an environmental health and safety officer, research safety officer, or biosecurity officer.
4. After approval, complete workspace setup via email. A payment card must be linked during activation. Then add members using the same school email domain and separately enable their GPT-Rosalind permissions.
5. The free period lasts up to 12 months, with a maximum of 30 seats per workspace. Lab members cannot apply individually. Pricing after the free period will follow the agreement in effect at the time of approval.
