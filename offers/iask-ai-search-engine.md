## 简介

iAsk 是带问答的 AI 搜索。学生完成身份验证后，可免费使用 Pro 一年。

## Description

iAsk is an AI-powered search with Q&A. Students can use the Pro version for free for one year after completing identity verification.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，注册或登录 iAsk。
2. 按页面完成学生验证。通过后 Pro 免费一年，以账户里显示的到期日为准。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page to register or log in to iAsk.
2. Complete the student verification on the page. Once approved, you'll receive one year of free Pro access, as indicated by the expiration date shown in your account.
