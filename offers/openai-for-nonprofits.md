## 简介

合格非营利组织购买 ChatGPT Business 标准席位可享优惠：年付约 $8/人/月，月付约 $10/人/月。高级席位不打折。Enterprise 可联系销售，最高约 75% 折扣。

## Description

Eligible nonprofits can get ChatGPT Business Standard seats at about $8 per user per month billed annually, or $10 monthly. Premium seats are excluded. Enterprise discounts of up to 75% go through sales.

## 如何领取

1. 按帮助中心表格用组织邮箱申请，由 Goodstack 核验。
2. 已有 Business 订阅的，通过后当前周期按比例折扣；新用户核验后在结账页只对标准席位打折。
3. API 目前没有非营利折扣。需要 BAA 要走 Enterprise 销售。

## How to claim

1. Apply with your organization email using the help center form; verified by Goodstack.
2. If you already have a Business subscription, after approval the current cycle is discounted on a prorated basis; for new users, after verification, only standard seats are discounted at checkout.
3. API currently has no nonprofit discount. If you need a BAA, go through Enterprise sales.
