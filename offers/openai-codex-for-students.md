## 简介

OpenAI 面向美国和加拿大在读大学生提供 $100 Codex 积分。通过 SheerID 验证学生身份后，积分自动添加到 ChatGPT 账户，可直接在 Codex 中使用，无需单独创建账号。

## Description

OpenAI offers $100 in Codex credits for verified university students in the United States and Canada. Complete student verification via SheerID and credits are automatically added to your ChatGPT account for use in Codex.

## 如何领取

1. 访问 [本页的](https://xn--8pvn55a0tz/)「前往官网领取」，点击「Claim now」
2. 使用或注册 ChatGPT 账号登录（无需学校邮箱关联）
3. 通过 SheerID 完成学生身份验证（提供姓名、院校、学生证等信息）
4. 验证成功后 $100 积分自动添加到 ChatGPT 账户
5. 前往 chatgpt.com/codex 即可使用，无需单独注册 Codex 账号

注意：仅限美国或加拿大在读大学生（居住地也须在美/加），每名学生限领一次。

## How to claim

1. Visit the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and click "Claim now"
2. Log in with or sign up for a ChatGPT account (no school email association required)
3. Complete student verification through SheerID (provide name, institution, student ID, etc.)
4. After successful verification, $100 in credits are automatically added to your ChatGPT account
5. Go to chatgpt.com/codex to use it; no separate Codex account registration required

Note: Only for current college students in the United States or Canada (residence must also be in the US/Canada). Limit one per student.
