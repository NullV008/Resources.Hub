## 简介

Genymotion Desktop 是 Android 模拟器。教育版每年 $49，按每名学生、每台电脑计，学生、教师和学校都能申请，要学生证或教师证等有效证明。功能和独立开发者年付 $239.99 那档一样，含全部 Pro 组件。免费个人版功能更少，没有最新 Android。云端 SaaS 不走这个教育价。

## Description

Genymotion Desktop is an Android emulator. The Education Edition costs $49 per year, per student and per computer. Students, teachers, and schools can apply, requiring valid proof such as a student ID or teacher ID. Its features are the same as the $239.99 annual plan for independent developers, including all Pro components. The free personal edition has fewer features and does not include the latest Android versions. The cloud-based SaaS version does not offer this educational pricing.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，在 Desktop 价格里选 Educational 申请。
2. 提交学生证或教师证等在读、任教证明，等审核。
3. 通过后再按年付费。不要选免费个人版，那个功能不全。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page, and select "Educational" when choosing a Desktop plan to apply.
2. Submit proof of enrollment or employment, such as a student ID or teacher ID, and wait for approval.
3. Pay the annual fee after approval. Do not choose the free personal edition, as it has limited features.
