## 简介

Otter.ai 是 AI 驱动的实时语音转文字与会议笔记工具，可自动生成课堂、会议的文字摘要与回放。学生教师折扣计划为合格的在校学生、教职人员及全职员工提供 Pro 个人月付/年付计划的专属折扣。适用机构包括非营利教育机构、经认证的中小学与高等院校及学区。使用学校邮箱（.edu 等）注册后，在升级页面选择"Student & Teacher Discount"即可享受折扣价结算。注意：折扣仅限个人 Pro 计划，不含 Workspace 协作功能。

## Description

Otter.ai is an AI-powered real-time speech-to-text and meeting notes tool that automatically generates transcripts, summaries, and playback for classes and meetings. The Student & Teacher discount program offers reduced pricing on the Pro individual monthly or annual plan for eligible students, faculty, and full-time staff at nonprofit educational institutions, accredited K-12 and higher education institutions, and school districts. Sign up with your school email (.edu), then select "Student & Teacher Discount" at the upgrade page. Note: Workspace features are not included; individual Pro plan only.

## 如何领取

1. 使用学校邮箱（如 xxx@university.edu）在 otter.ai 注册免费 Basic 账号。2. 登录后点击左上角头像 → "Upgrade plan"。3. 在方案栏下方点击"Student & Teacher Discount"链接。4. 按提示完成付款（仅支持信用卡），折扣将自动在结算时应用。如邮箱域名不含.edu，可联系 Otter 支持团队提交学校认证文件进行人工审核。注意：须先退出 Workspace 才能使用此折扣。

## How to claim

1. Use a school email (e.g., xxx@university.edu) to sign up for a free Basic account at otter.ai.
2. After logging in, click the avatar in the upper-left corner → "Upgrade plan".
3. Below the plan section, click the "Student & Teacher Discount" link.
4. Follow the prompts to complete payment (credit card only); the discount will be automatically applied at checkout. If the email domain does not include.edu, contact Otter support to submit school verification documents for manual review. Note: You must sign out of Workspace first to use this discount.
