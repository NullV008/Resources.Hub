## 简介

Speechify 是文本转语音与语音 AI 助手，可朗读 PDF、网页和课本，支持 200+ 人声、可调倍速和 AI 摘要。教育版面向学校与学生；验证学生通常可通过 UNiDAYS / Student Beans 获 Premium 约 25% 折扣，美国 K-12 可申请免费 Premium。

## Description

Speechify is a text-to-speech and voice AI assistant that can read PDFs, web pages, and textbooks, with 200+ voices, adjustable speed, and AI summaries. The education offering is for schools and students; verified students typically get about 25% off Premium via UNiDAYS or Student Beans, and US K-12 students may qualify for free Premium.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」了解教育版，或先下载 iOS / Android / 网页 / Chrome 插件试用免费版。
2. 大学生：通过 UNiDAYS 或 Student Beans 完成学生认证后领取 Premium 约 25% 折扣（不是结账输入优惠码）。
3. 美国 K-12 学生可按教育计划申请免费 Premium。
4. 学校或学区批量采购请通过教育页申请演示/联系销售。价格以官方页面为准。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page to learn about the education edition, or first download the iOS / Android / web / Chrome extension to try the free version.
2. University students: complete student verification via UNiDAYS or Student Beans to get about 25% off Premium (not by entering a promo code at checkout).
3. US K-12 students can apply for free Premium through the education program.
4. Schools or districts buying in bulk should request a demo/contact sales via the education page. Prices are subject to the official page.
