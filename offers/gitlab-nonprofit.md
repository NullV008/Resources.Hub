## 简介

已在当地政府注册、状态正常的非营利组织，可免费获得一年 GitLab Ultimate，云端或自建都可以，最多 20 个席位。不含技术支持。每年名额先到先得，捐助额度用完就停止申请。政治或宗教导向的组织通常不能参加。和面向学校的 GitLab 教育计划不是同一项。

## Description

Nonprofit organizations that are registered with their local government and in good standing can receive a free one-year GitLab Ultimate license, either in the cloud or self-hosted, for up to 20 seats. Technical support is not included. Annual spots are available on a first-come, first-served basis, and applications will close once the donation quota is reached. Organizations with political or religious affiliations are generally not eligible. This is separate from the GitLab Education program for schools.

## 如何领取

1. 确认组织已注册且状态正常。超过 20 个席位不要在页面申请，改发邮件到 nonprofits@gitlab.com。
2. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，由组织联系人提交。GoodStack 会核验。
3. 通过后按邮件领取许可。每年续期必须由当初在客户门户创建订阅的同一个人操作。

## How to claim

1. Confirm that your organization is registered and in good standing. If you have more than 20 seats, do not apply through this page; instead, email nonprofits@gitlab.com.
2. Click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page, and have the organization's contact person submit the application. GoodStack will verify it.
3. Once approved, receive your license via email. Annual renewals must be handled by the same person who originally created the subscription in the customer portal.
