## 简介

正在学关系型或 NoSQL 数据库的学生，可以用真实大学邮箱免费申请 DBeaver Enterprise 或 CloudBeaver 学术许可。审核通过后许可发到邮箱，一般不超过 3 个工作日。

## Description

Students studying relational or NoSQL databases can apply for a free DBeaver Enterprise or CloudBeaver academic license using their official university email address. Once approved, the license will be sent to your email, typically within 3 business days.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用大学邮箱填写申请，并写明在学的数据库课程或方向。
2. 等审核邮件。通过后按邮件里的许可安装，不要先买商业版。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page, fill out the application with your university email, and specify the database course or specialization you are currently studying.
2. Wait for the approval email. After approval, follow the instructions in the email to install the license. Do not purchase the commercial version beforehand.
