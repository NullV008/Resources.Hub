## 简介

think-cell 是 PowerPoint 图表插件。在读学生可以申请免费许可，用来做课程演示。

## Description

think-cell is a PowerPoint chart add-in. Current students can apply for a free license to use in course presentations.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱填写学生许可申请。
2. 准备好在读证明。许可发到邮箱后，再按邮件安装。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and fill out the student license application using your school email.
2. Have proof of enrollment ready. After the license is sent to your email, follow the instructions in the email to install it.
