## 简介

师生可免费使用 Excalidraw 白板。用教育邮箱注册后，Excalidraw+ 有教育价（法文官方页写明约 $4/月或 $36/年），以结账页为准。

## Description

Students and teachers can use the Excalidraw whiteboard for free. An educational email unlocks reduced Excalidraw+ pricing (the French official page lists about $4/month or $36/year). Confirm the amount at checkout.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 免费白板可直接使用。
3. 需要 Plus（云端收藏、语音、PDF/PPTX 导出等）时，用教育邮箱注册并继续到结账，或完成身份核验后再看教育价。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page.
2. The free whiteboard can be used directly.
3. If you need Plus (cloud favorites, voice, PDF/PPTX export, etc.), sign up with an education email and continue to checkout, or complete identity verification to see the education price.
