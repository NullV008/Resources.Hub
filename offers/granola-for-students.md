## 简介

美国、英国和加拿大认证高校的在读生（含博士）可申请 12 个月免费的 Granola Business，把课堂录音整理成可搜索的笔记。其他国家不在这次活动范围内。

## Description

Currently enrolled students (including doctoral students) at accredited universities in the United States, United Kingdom, and Canada can apply for 12 months of free Granola Business to transcribe classroom recordings into searchable notes. This promotion is not available in other countries.

## 如何领取

确认学校在美国、英国或加拿大。
打开页面申请 12 个月 Granola Business，并按要求验证在读身份。

## How to claim

Confirm that your school is located in the United States, United Kingdom, or Canada.
Open the page to apply for 12 months of Granola Business and verify your current student status as required.
