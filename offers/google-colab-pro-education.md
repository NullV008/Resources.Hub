## 简介

用教育邮箱可以免费使用 Colab Pro 一年，比免费版有更好的 GPU 和更长的运行时间。一年结束后不会自动再送，要以当时的教育活动为准。

## Description

You can use Colab Pro for free for one year with an educational email account, which offers better GPUs and longer runtime compared to the free version. The free access will not be automatically renewed after the year ends; eligibility will depend on ongoing educational promotions at that time.

## 如何领取

1. 用教育邮箱对应的 Google 账号点击本页的「前往官网领取」。
2. 按页面开通一年 Pro。看清楚到期日，到期后会回到免费版或要求付费。

## How to claim

1. Use the Google account associated with your educational email to click "Go to the official website to claim" on this page.
2. Follow the page instructions to activate the one-year Pro subscription. Pay attention to the expiration date, as your account will revert to the free version or require payment after it expires.
