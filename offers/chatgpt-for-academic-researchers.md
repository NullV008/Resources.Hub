## 简介

OpenAI 面向高校教职人员和博士后研究人员推出的专属计划，审核通过后可免费获得 12 个月的专属 ChatGPT 工作空间。每个工作空间最多支持 5 名成员（申请人及最多 4 名同机构协作者），享有 ChatGPT Pro 级别的使用配额、最新前沿模型访问权限，并配备企业级数据隐私保护（数据默认不用于训练模型）。申请时需通过 SheerID 验证机构隶属关系、提交代表性论文及研究用途说明。

## Description

OpenAI's exclusive program for university faculty and postdoctoral researchers. Approved applicants receive 12 months of complimentary access to a dedicated ChatGPT workspace with ChatGPT Pro-level usage, the latest frontier models, and business-grade data privacy protections (data not used for training by default). Each workspace supports up to 5 members — the applicant plus up to 4 verified collaborators from the same institution. Applicants verify institutional affiliation via SheerID and submit a qualifying paper with a brief description of intended research use.

## 如何领取

1. 访问本页的「前往官网领取」查看最新申请入口与资格要求。
2. 使用学校/机构邮箱登录 OpenAI 账号。
3. 通过 SheerID 完成学术机构隶属关系验证。
4. 提交一篇符合条件的代表性论文及研究用途简短说明。
5. 审核通过后以 $0 完成结算（需绑定有效信用卡），即可创建专属研究工作空间。
6. 工作空间建好后，可邀请最多 4 名同机构成员加入。

注意：本计划仅面向教职人员和博士后研究人员，提供 ChatGPT 访问权限（非 API 额度）。

## How to claim

1. Visit the "Get it on the official site" button on this page to view the latest application entry and eligibility requirements.
2. Sign in to your OpenAI account using your school/institution email.
3. Complete academic institution affiliation verification through SheerID.
4. Submit one eligible representative paper and a brief description of your research use.
5. After approval, complete checkout at $0 (a valid credit card is required) to create your dedicated research workspace.
6. Once the workspace is set up, you can invite up to 4 members from the same institution to join.

Note: This program is only for faculty and postdoctoral researchers and provides ChatGPT access (not API credits).
