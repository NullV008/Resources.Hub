## 简介

OpenASI 是面向吉林大学师生的非营利开放平台，由吉大学生初创企业 BitMiracle AI Lab 全资赞助，免费提供限量的前沿模型算力。注册需要 @mails.jlu.edu.cn 或 @jlu.edu.cn 邮箱，以及邀请码。

## Description

OpenASI is a non-profit open platform for students and faculty at Jilin University, fully sponsored by BitMiracle AI Lab, a startup founded by Jilin University students. It provides a limited amount of free computing power for cutting-edge models. Registration requires an email address from @mails.jlu.edu.cn or @jlu.edu.cn, as well as an invitation code.

## 如何领取

1. 打开注册页，用 @mails.jlu.edu.cn 或 @jlu.edu.cn 邮箱创建账号。
2. 填写邀请码。还没有邀请码时，加入 QQ 群 1007610343 获取。
3. 登录后在额度内使用前沿模型。名额和额度有限，以平台当时说明为准。

## How to claim

1. Open the registration page and create an account using an email from @mails.jlu.edu.cn or @jlu.edu.cn.
2. Enter your invitation code. If you don't have one yet, join the QQ group 1007610343 to obtain one.
3. After logging in, use the cutting-edge models within your allocated quota. The number of spots and available quota are limited, subject to the platform's current terms.
