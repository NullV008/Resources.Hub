**NVIDIA Academic Research Grant**

## 简介

NVIDIA 和高校研究者合作，给授予博士学位的高校全职教师提供云端 GPU 时长、硬件或软件，以及 NVIDIA 模型。方向包括仿真建模、生成式训练、推理与智能体，资源可以到 3 万小时 H100，或若干 RTX PRO / DGX Spark。提案必须使用 NVIDIA 预训练模型或软件栈，并按当轮征集说明和模板来写。全球可交，竞争性录取，不是本科生个人优惠。官方目前不接受新申请。

## Description

NVIDIA collaborates with university researchers to provide cloud GPU time, hardware, software, and NVIDIA models to full-time faculty members at universities that award doctoral degrees. Focus areas include simulation and modeling, generative training, inference, and agents. Resources can include up to 30,000 hours of H100 time or several RTX PRO / DGX Spark units. Proposals must utilize NVIDIA's pre-trained models or software stack and be written according to the guidelines and template for the current call. Applications are open globally, with competitive selection; this is not a personal benefit for undergraduate students. Officially, new applications are currently not being accepted.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，先看当轮是否开放以及对应的征集说明。
2. 须是授予博士学位高校的全职教师。按提案模板提交，不符合模板的不审。
3. 获批后按批准的云端时长或硬件使用。上轮获奖者要先交结果，才能再申请。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Apply" on this page to check if the current call is open and review the corresponding guidelines.
2. Applicants must be full-time faculty members at universities that award doctoral degrees. Submissions must follow the proposal template; applications that do not comply will not be reviewed.
3. Upon approval, use the approved cloud time or hardware as specified. Previous round winners must first submit their results before applying again.
