## 简介

Vercel 的 AI 应用生成工具。目前只对页面列出的在读院校开放。用该校学生邮箱完成验证后，可免费使用 v0 Premium 一年。名单里有斯坦福、哈佛、普林斯顿、加州大学伯克利分校、麻省理工、纽约大学、滑铁卢大学等，并会继续增加。学校不在名单里时可以加入等候名单，同校报名的人越多，越有机会被加进去。其他学生仍可使用 v0 免费版。

## Description

Vercel's AI application generation tool. Currently available only to students at the institutions listed on the page. After verifying your identity with a student email from one of these schools, you can use v0 Premium for free for one year. The list includes Stanford, Harvard, Princeton, University of California, Berkeley, MIT, New York University, University of Waterloo, and more, with additional schools being added regularly. If your school isn't on the list, you can join the waitlist—the more students from your school who sign up, the higher the chance it will be added. Students from other schools can still use the free version of v0.

## 如何领取

1. 打开 [本页的](https://xn--8pvn55a0tz/)「前往官网领取」，查看自己的学校是否在 Active Schools 里。
2. 用该校学生邮箱注册或登录，按页面完成学生验证。
3. 验证通过后获得一年 v0 Premium。学校不在名单中就加入等候名单。

## How to claim

1. Visit the "Get it on the official site" button on this page to check if your school is listed under Active Schools.
2. Register or log in using your student email from the eligible school and complete the student verification process as instructed on the page.
3. Once verified, you'll receive one year of v0 Premium. If your school isn't on the list, join the waitlist.
