## 简介

学生和教师购买 Xmind 可走教育价。教育页展示 Premium 约 $3.42/月（另有划线价），需提交在读或在职证明，以结算页为准。

## Description

Students and educators can buy Xmind at the education price. The education page shows Premium at about $3.42/month. Proof of status is required; confirm the price at checkout.

## 如何领取

1. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」选择教育方案。
2. 提交学生证、在读证明、课表、教师工作证明或.edu 邮箱等材料。
3. 审核通过后再付款。提供虚假材料会被取消资格。

## How to claim

1. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and choose an education plan.
2. Submit materials such as a student ID, proof of enrollment, class schedule, teacher employment proof, or .edu email.
3. Pay after approval. Submitting false materials will result in disqualification.
