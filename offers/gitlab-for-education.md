## 简介

合格非营利学校可由全职教师或职员申请免费 GitLab Ultimate（不限席位，含每月 5 万计算分钟），用于教学和非商业科研。许可不直接发给学生。中国机构需联系极狐。

## Description

Qualifying nonprofit schools can get free GitLab Ultimate (unlimited seats, 50,000 compute minutes a month) for teaching and non-commercial research. Only full-time faculty or staff can apply. Licenses are not issued to students. Institutions in China should contact JiHu.

## 如何领取

1. 由本校全职教师或职员用学校邮箱打开本页的「前往官网领取」join/ 申请。
2. 学校需受认可、以教学为主、能发学历且为非营利。通过 Proxi.id 核验。
3. 学生不能个人领取，请让老师申请，或使用 GitLab 免费版。
4. 中国机构官方不发此许可，联系极狐 ychen@gitlab.cn。许可一年，可按年续。

## How to claim

1. A full-time faculty member or staff member at the school applies by opening the "Get it on the official site" button on this pagejoin/ with a school email address.
2. The school must be accredited, primarily teaching-focused, able to award degrees, and nonprofit. Verification is done through Proxi.id.
3. Students cannot obtain it individually; ask a teacher to apply, or use GitLab Free.
4. The license is not officially issued to institutions in China; contact JiHu at ychen@gitlab.cn. The license lasts one year and can be renewed annually.
