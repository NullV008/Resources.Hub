## 简介

Zed 是带 AI 的协作代码编辑器。大学生验证通过后，可免费使用 Zed Pro 一年，包含每月 $10 AI 额度，以及无限次编辑预测。

## Description

Zed is a collaborative code editor with AI. After verification, university students can use Zed Pro for free for one year, including a monthly $10 AI credit and unlimited editing suggestions.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用大学邮箱登录 Zed。
2. 完成学生认证。通过后看账户里是否出现一年 Pro 和每月 $10 额度。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and log in to Zed with your university email.
2. Complete student verification. Once approved, check your account to see if the one-year Pro subscription and monthly $10 credit have been added.
