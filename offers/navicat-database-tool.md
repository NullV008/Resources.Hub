## 简介

在受认证的非营利高中、大学或社区学院就读，并持有有效学校邮箱的学生，可申请一年免费、不可转售的 Navicat Premium、Data Modeler、Monitor 非商业版和 Navicat Cloud Pro。培训机构和非认证学校不能申请。每人只能申请一次，只能用于学习，不能交给第三方使用或谋利。

## Description

Students enrolled in an accredited non-profit high school, university, or community college and holding a valid school email address may apply for a free, non-transferable one-year license to Navicat Premium, Data Modeler, Monitor Non-Commercial Edition, and Navicat Cloud Pro. Training institutions and non-accredited schools are not eligible. Each person may apply only once, and the license may be used solely for educational purposes, not transferred to third parties or used for profit.

## 如何领取

1. 打开学生申请页，填写 NAPP 申请表，并使用学校邮箱。
2. 确认学校是受认证的非营利高中、大学或社区学院。
3. 审核通过后获得一年许可。同一人不能再次申请。

## How to claim

1. Open the student application page, fill out the NAPP application form, and use your school email address.
2. Confirm that your school is an accredited non-profit high school, university, or community college.
3. Upon approval, you will receive a one-year license. The same individual cannot apply again.
