## 简介

Kiro 是 AWS 推出的 AI 驱动代码编辑器，支持规格驱动开发与自主 Agent。通过大学学生认证后，可免费获得每月 1,000 积分，持续一年，无需信用卡。目前支持部分精选大学，使用大学邮箱注册后通过 SheerID 验证。

## Description

Kiro is an AI-powered code editor by AWS featuring spec-driven development and autonomous agents. Verified university students get 1,000 credits per month free for one year — no credit card required. Sign up with your university email and verify via SheerID.

## 如何领取

1. 使用大学邮箱注册 AWS Builder ID（本页的「前往官网领取」）
2. 登录后进入账户使用页面，若学校在支持列表中会看到"You are eligible for Kiro Students"横幅
3. 点击 Verify 按钮，通过 SheerID 完成学生身份验证（需提供姓名、生日、大学信息）
4. 验证成功后立即获得每月 1,000 积分，有效期一年
5. 当前支持学校：ASU、Cal Poly、CMU、Georgia Tech、NYU、U of Toronto、U of Waterloo 等

## How to claim

1. Register for an AWS Builder ID with your university email (the "Get it on the official site" button on this page)
2. After logging in, go to the account usage page. If your school is on the supported list, you'll see a "You are eligible for Kiro Students" banner
3. Click the Verify button and complete student verification through SheerID (you'll need to provide your name, birthday, and university information)
4. After successful verification, you immediately get 1,000 credits per month for one year
5. Currently supported schools: ASU, Cal Poly, CMU, Georgia Tech, NYU, U of Toronto, U of Waterloo, etc.
