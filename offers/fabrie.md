## 简介

Fabrie 是在线协作白板。学生可以申请教育优惠，具体折扣和时长以价格页当前活动为准。

## Description

Fabrie is an online collaborative whiteboard. Students can apply for an educational discount. The specific discount and duration are subject to the current promotion on the pricing page.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用学校邮箱登录。
2. 在价格页找到学生方案并按提示验证。通过前不要买普通席位。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page and log in with your school email.
2. Find the student plan on the pricing page and follow the prompts to verify. Do not purchase a regular plan before verification is complete.
