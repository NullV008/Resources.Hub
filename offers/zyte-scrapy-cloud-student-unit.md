GitHub Student Developer Pack members get 1 Scrapy Cloud unit free for as long as they qualify: unlimited team members, projects, and crawl time, with 120-day data retention.

1. First verify your student status through GitHub Student Developer Pack.
2. Open the "Get it on the official site" button on this page and claim it through the Pack entry.
3. The benefit is 1 permanently free unit. For questions, email support@zyte.com.
