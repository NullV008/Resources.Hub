## 简介

Cursor 从 2026 年 6 月 25 日起停止新的学生优惠注册。以前领到的人可以维持到期；没领过的不能再拿一年免费 Pro，也没有五折。本科生要等校园或线上活动的额度，硕士、博士、研究者和教师只能填表申请额度。

## Description

Cursor will stop accepting new student discount registrations starting June 25, 2026. Those who previously received the discount can continue using it until its expiration. Those who have not yet received it can no longer get a year of free Pro access or a 50% discount. Undergraduate students should wait for quotas available through campus or online events, while master's and doctoral students, researchers, and teachers can only apply for quotas by filling out a form.

## 如何领取

新的学生优惠已经结束。已经领过的，到期前到账单页取消，否则会按原方案续费。不要再按旧教程做学生认证。

## How to claim

The new student discount has ended. If you have already received it, cancel it on the billing page before it expires; otherwise, it will be renewed under the original plan. Do not follow the old tutorial to complete student verification anymore.
