**Quark Student Verification**

## 简介

夸克的学生和教师认证通过后，AI 会员、网盘会员和扫描王会员各送 3 个月。三个会员要分别到账，不是一个会员通吃所有功能。

## Description

After Quark students and teachers complete verification, they will receive 3 months free for each of the following: AI membership, cloud storage membership, and Scan King membership. These three memberships will be credited separately; one membership does not grant access to all features.

## 如何领取

1. 点击本页的「前往官网领取」，用夸克账号登录并选择学生或老师。
2. 按页面完成认证。通过后分别查看 AI、网盘和扫描王的到期日。

## How to claim

1. Click "Go to Official Website to Claim" on this page, log in with your Quark account, and select whether you are a student or teacher.
2. Complete the verification process as instructed on the page. Once verified, check the expiration dates for your AI, cloud storage, and Scan King memberships separately.
