**Lovable Pro 50% Off for Students**

## 简介

认证高校学生（及教师）可享 Lovable Pro 五折，例如 100 credits 方案约 $12.50/月，最长 12 个月。用学校邮箱或学生证验证，仅限当前免费套餐用户。

## Description

Verified students (and teachers) get 50% off Lovable Pro — e.g. the 100-credit plan about $12.50/mo — for up to 12 months. Verify with a school email or student ID. Free-plan accounts only.

## 如何领取

1. 用学校邮箱登录 Lovable（需仍是免费套餐）。
2. 打开本页的「前往官网领取」，点 Verify Student Status，用学校邮箱或学生证核验。全球多数认证高校可用。
3. 通过后按学生价订阅。折扣最长 12 个月；取消后再订无法重新激活该折扣。

教师也可用学术邮箱领取学生价；若要给整班开通，可看 lovable.dev/partners/education。

## How to claim

1. Sign in to Lovable with your school email (you must still be on the free plan).
2. Go to the "Get it on the official site" button on this page, click Verify Student Status, and verify with your school email or student ID. Available at most accredited universities worldwide.
3. After approval, subscribe at the student price. The discount lasts up to 12 months; if you cancel and resubscribe, you cannot reactivate the discount.

Teachers can also get the student price with an academic email; to set it up for a whole class, see lovable.dev/partners/education.
